Account

How do I create a Gamer's HUB account?

Creating your Gamer's HUB account takes less than 2 minutes. Follow these steps to get online:

1

Visit gamershub.gg/signup or click the Sign In button in the top-right corner of any page.

2

Enter your email address, choose a unique gamer tag, and set a strong password (min. 8 characters).

3

Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link within 24 hours.

4

Complete your Neural Profile — add your gaming preferences, region, and avatar to unlock full community access.

5

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) immediately from Settings → Security to protect your account.

Tip: Use a unique email and never share your password. Gamer's HUB staff will never ask for your password.
Games

How do I download and install a game?

After purchasing a game, it's added to your HUB Library automatically. Here's how to install it:

1

Go to My Library from your profile dashboard. All purchased and free games appear here.

2

Click the game title, then press the Install button. Choose your preferred installation directory.

3

The HUB Launcher will handle the download. You can track progress in the Downloads tab at the bottom of the launcher.

4

Once complete, click Play. The launcher verifies game integrity before launch to ensure you have a clean build.

5

If you encounter errors, use Verify Game Files (right-click game → Properties) to auto-repair corrupted files.

Tip: Ensure you have at least 10% free space on your drive beyond the game's listed size for smooth installation.
Payments

How do I request a refund for a purchase?

Gamer's HUB offers refunds under the following conditions:

Request made within 14 days of purchase.
Game has been played for less than 2 hours.
Physical goods are unopened and undamaged.
DLC and in-game currency are non-refundable once redeemed.
1

Go to My Orders in your account dashboard and find the relevant purchase.

2

Click Request Refund, select the reason from the dropdown, and add any additional details.

3

Submit the form. Refund decisions are processed within 3–5 business days and credited back to your original payment method.

Security

How do I recover a hacked or lost account?

If you suspect unauthorized access, act immediately:

1

Don't panic. Visit the Account Recovery Portal — you don't need to be logged in.

2

Enter your registered email. A secure recovery link is sent to your inbox. Check your spam folder if it doesn't appear.

3

Verify your identity using one of: backup email, phone number, or your last 4 digits of the payment method used.

4

Set a new strong password and immediately enable or reset your 2FA. Review Active Sessions and log out of all unknown devices.

5

Report the breach via Security → Report Compromise so our team can investigate suspicious activity on your account.

Warning: If recovery fails, contact support immediately with your account email and a photo ID. We will never ask for your password.
Community

How do I report another player or bad conduct?

We take community conduct seriously. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team within 48 hours.

1

Navigate to the offending player's profile page or hover over their username in any chat or forum post.

2

Click the ⋮ Options menu → select Report Player.

3

Choose the violation type: Harassment, Cheating, Hate Speech, Spam, Impersonation, or Other. Add a description and attach screenshots if available.

4

Submit. You'll receive a confirmation and can track status under My Reports in your dashboard.

5

You can also use Block Player to immediately stop any interaction from that user while the review is ongoing.

Note: False reports are themselves a conduct violation. Please only report genuine issues.
